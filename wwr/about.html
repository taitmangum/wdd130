Canyon Rush Rafting

Canyon Rush Rafting exists to help people experience the excitement of the outdoors through safe and memorable river adventures. Our guides are passionate about creating trips that build confidence, friendships, and lifelong memories.

History

Canyon Rush Rafting began with a small group of outdoor enthusiasts who loved exploring rivers throughout the western United States. What started as weekend adventures eventually became a company dedicated to sharing those experiences with others.
