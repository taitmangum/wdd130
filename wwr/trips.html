Half Day Adventure

A great choice for first-time rafters or groups with a busy schedule. This trip includes scenic canyon views, fun splashy rapids, and enough excitement to make the day memorable without taking up your whole schedule.

Big Water Rush

Our full-day trip is built for guests who want a longer river experience with bigger water, more paddling, and a riverside lunch. It is a strong option for families, friend groups, and anyone ready for a full adventure.

Rapid Ridge Run

This trip is designed for guests looking for a more intense white water experience. With stronger rapids and tighter canyon sections, Rapid Ridge Run is best for adventurous groups who are ready to paddle hard and get wet.
